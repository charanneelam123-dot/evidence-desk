# Fictional Northstar employee handbook

Employees receive 20 days of paid vacation per calendar year. Unused vacation may carry over up to 5 days.

Remote work is permitted three days per week. Managers approve schedules in advance.

Learning reimbursement covers up to 1000 USD per year for approved courses. Submit receipts within 30 days.

Security incidents must be reported to the security team immediately. Never share passwords or API keys.
